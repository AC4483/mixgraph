# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-02)

First public release.

- **Next**: see your logged transitions out of the playing track, plus untried tracks with a compatible Camelot key and BPM within 6%.
- **Practice**: To try / Learning / Nailed status per transition, a clean-or-messy practice log, and a "Practice today" list.
- **Sets**: build a set from a start track, length and energy shape; energy curve, readiness and gaps; swap, reorder and play it live.
- **Graph**: tracks placed around the Camelot wheel, colored by key or energy, with arrows for transitions.
- **Tracks**: library with title, artist, BPM, key and energy (1–10).
- Data is saved in your browser's localStorage.
